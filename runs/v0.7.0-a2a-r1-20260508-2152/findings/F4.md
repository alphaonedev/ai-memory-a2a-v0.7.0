# F4 — RESOLVED via scope manifest skip_3_agent

**Status:** RESOLVED in Round 1 (Path C topology bring-up)
**Severity:** P0 (carryover from prior failed Round 1)
**Affected:** S14, S25, S39

## Prior failure

S14 (`14_partition_tolerance.py`) sent `kill -STOP` to NODE3's daemon and
expected NODE1+NODE2 to keep working with W=2 (alice+bob = quorum). With
NODE3_IP aliased to NODE2_IP in the v0.7.0 2-droplet topology, the
SIGSTOP froze hermes; the runner timed out at 109s and SIGKILLed the
scenario before it could SIGCONT, leaving hermes paused. That cascaded
into "infrastructure blocked" SKIPs across S16-S69 (everything that ran
after S14).

## Resolution

`scripts/scope-v0.7.0.json` lists S14, S25, S39 in `skip_3_agent` — the
runner reads the manifest at startup and emits a clean SKIP record
WITHOUT executing those scenarios. Confirmed in this Round 1: each
emitted

    {"skipped": true, "skip_reason": "...requires a real third daemon..."}

without freezing hermes. Hermes remained healthy through the entire
971-second run; subsequent scenarios executed normally.

The S14 / S25 / S39 oracles legitimately need three distinct daemons to
validate (suspend ONE peer; the other two must keep accepting writes).
Re-introduction is gated on adding a third ai-memory droplet — deferred
to v0.7.1 per the campaign scope JSON.

## Defense-in-depth backstop

`Harness.from_env(scenario_id, require_node3=True)` is now wired up in
`scripts/a2a_harness.py`. If a future scenario opts in to `require_node3=True`
and the env shows NODE3 aliased to NODE2, the harness emits SKIP cleanly
via stdout JSON and exits 0 (no SIGSTOP, no infrastructure block).

No further action required.
